Partner FAQ

What is an AUTOCPR center?

An AI-powered, low-staff community emergency training center developed by ALLCPR. It uses an AI Agent operating platform, smart manikins and a standardized teaching process to deliver CPR, AED, First Aid and BLS training and certification.

How much space does an AUTOCPR center need?

About 15–20 m², roughly 160–215 sq ft.

Where can an AUTOCPR center be located?

In commercial buildings, community centers, schools, fitness facilities and other public-service locations.

How does a center run with few staff?

Learners register, schedule and complete online coursework on the digital platform. An intelligent training system guides the in-person skills session and records performance data.

Who can partner with ALLCPR on AUTOCPR?

ALLCPR works with nonprofit organizations, schools, businesses and individuals through project partnerships, corporate collaborations and authorized franchise models.

How much does it cost to open an AUTOCPR center?

Costs depend on the partnership type and location. The ALLCPR partnership team shares details on the intro call after you apply.

How do I apply?

Fill in the AUTOCPR partner application. You get a confirmation email with next steps, and the ALLCPR partnership team follows up to schedule an intro call.
