AUTOCPR FAQ

Common questions about AUTOCPR by ALLCPR

Is AUTOCPR a CPR machine?

No. AUTOCPR by ALLCPR is a community CPR training center brand. It is not a mechanical chest compression device such as LUCAS or AutoPulse.

Who runs AUTOCPR?

ALLCPR, a U.S. CPR training network founded in 2022 and headquartered in San Jose, California.

Which certifications can I get at AUTOCPR?

CPR, AED, First Aid and BLS. ALLCPR delivers American Red Cross programs within the Red Cross PLTP partnership framework and AHA BLS Provider courses.

How fast do I get my certification card?

ALLCPR courses issue same-day or next-day eCards, depending on the course and the issuing organization.

Where are AUTOCPR centers?

San Jose, Santa Clara and Newark, California. San Francisco's first ARC AUTOCPR center is in development. See locations.

Can my company book group training?

Yes. Groups of 8 or more can request onsite CPR, First Aid or BLS training through ALLCPR group training.

How do I open an AUTOCPR center?

Apply on the AUTOCPR partner page. ALLCPR partners through project partnerships, corporate collaborations and authorized franchise models.
