ONPECY and AUTOCPR

ONPECY teaches people to run an AI One Person Company, and AUTOCPR is one of its real-world projects.

The ONPECY Global Entrepreneurship Community launched on July 20, 2026, co-founded by Gosvea Inc., Minto, ALLCPR and partners from Singapore. Learners practice on real projects such as AUTOCPR community CPR training centers.

ONPECY certification levels

LevelFor

Specialized tracks include AI Agent Development, AI Automation, Digital Employees, AI Marketing, Physical AI and AI Healthcare.

From learner to AUTOCPR partner

ONPECY learners can apply for AUTOCPR partnership after their training, using the same partner application.

ONPECY certifications are professional certifications, not academic degrees. Results depend on each person's own work, and no income is promised.
