What is an ALTP?

An Associate Licensed Training Provider signs an ALTP agreement directly with the Red Cross and teaches under its own business name. Its Red Cross account is linked to ALLCPR’s Primary account; ALLCPR enters class records and pays the applicable Red Cross invoices.

Who can join?

Red Cross–certified instructors operating their own training business, subject to application review, Red Cross approval and onboarding. There is no class minimum.

Do I need an LLC?

No. Sole proprietors, LLCs, corporations and organizations can apply.

What does it cost?

You pay course-specific prepaid credits, a monthly Plan fee and a one-time setup fee. For Founding Partners signing by December 31, 2026, the $199 setup fee and monthly Plan fees through that date are waived. The Plan fee is currently estimated at $25 from 2027. See Pricing for conditions and exclusions.

What if I have no classes for a while?

There is no class minimum and no charge for classes you do not teach. The monthly Plan fee still applies when due, even if you teach no classes that month.

What is included in the per student price?

The Red Cross charge and ALLCPR’s record-entry service. Blended-course prices include the online part.

What is not included?

Students, venue, equipment, insurance, advertising and Class Posting charges, student-payment processing fees, travel, supplies and applicable taxes.

How do credits work?

Buy course-specific credits before teaching. They become usable after payment confirmation. Each attendee uses one credit regardless of result; no-shows and cancelled classes use none. Credits expire when their course version is retired or five years after purchase, whichever comes first.

Can I get a refund on credits?

Usually no, including if you leave. Limited exceptions cover duplicate or erroneous charges, certain endings not caused by you, and a course discontinued without a replacement. See Pricing and Schedule 6 of the Partner Agreement.

Can prices change?

Yes, with at least 30 days’ written notice. Credits already purchased keep their price.

Do I need insurance?

Yes. Carry at least $1 million general liability coverage per occurrence for CPR and first aid training, with coverage in place before your Red Cross ALTP agreement takes effect. Red Cross insurance does not cover you or your instructors.

How long until I can teach?

The Red Cross step usually takes one to two weeks, but approval and timing are its decision. Teaching also requires an effective ALTP agreement, verified account configuration, completed onboarding and approved first-class readiness. Account activation alone is not enough.

What do you need before my first class?

Insurance details and proof, your certified instructor list, your activated Red Cross account number, card-on-file authorization, and completion of the agreed onboarding and readiness steps. Course credits must be purchased separately and payment confirmed; the saved card does not automatically buy credits.

I am already a Red Cross LTP Will there be a gap?

We cannot guarantee a gap-free transition. With your permission, we ask the Red Cross about the switch and send a written plan for your approval before requesting an account change.

Do you find students for me?

No. You manage student acquisition and your own business. We help with Red Cross Class Posting listings and explain ways to be found. We do not promise leads, enrollment, income or profit.

What do I do after a class?

Record the complete roster in the class-registration system and submit it in the Partner Portal as soon as possible, no later than 11:59 p.m. at the class location on the third calendar day after the class ends. Include actual attendance and results. Our normal entry target is two business days after completeness, verification and paid-credit coverage are confirmed.

What if I do not have enough credits?

We notify you within one business day after roster submission. Buy the shortfall within two business days after that notice, or sooner if a Red Cross deadline requires. We preserve and track submitted records but do not enter unpaid records. A shortfall does not extend a Red Cross deadline.

How do I add an instructor?

Add the qualified instructor to your Red Cross account and tell us at least two business days before their first class so we can add them to the registration system. Each instructor must hold the appropriate current certification and be authorized by your business.

How do I get help?

Email partners@allcpr.org. Our reply commitment is one business day under the staffed calendar provided at onboarding. Evening, weekend and class-day phone coverage must be confirmed separately in writing. This administrative service is not an emergency line.

How do I leave?

Give written notice at any time, with no termination fee. No Plan fee is charged for months beginning after your notice. The end date is at least 30 days after our notice to the Red Cross. To continue teaching Red Cross courses afterward, arrange another applicable Red Cross agreement or Primary-provider relationship.

What happens to my credits and records when I leave?

Credits may cover classes taught before your end date and rosters submitted within 30 days of your notice; unused credits then expire unless a refund exception applies. We reconcile pending work and provide a final statement and a spreadsheet of your student and class history under the agreement. Leaving does not erase pending records.
