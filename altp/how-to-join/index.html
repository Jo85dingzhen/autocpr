ALTP Home / How to join

A clear path to your first class.

Find out what you need, how the application works and when you can start teaching.

What you need

Operate under your own business name as a sole proprietor, company or organization. An LLC is not required.

Use instructors with current Red Cross instructor certification for each course they teach, in good standing with the Red Cross and authorized by your business before teaching.

Carry general liability insurance of at least $1 million per occurrence covering CPR and first aid training. Provide proof during onboarding and have the required coverage in place before your Red Cross ALTP agreement takes effect. Red Cross insurance does not cover you or your instructors.

Provide suitable equipment and a venue, manage your own students, and follow the applicable Red Cross course, recordkeeping and branding requirements.

Before your first class, provide your insurance details, instructor list, activated Red Cross account number and card-on-file authorization. Enter card details only through the payment provider’s secure page, never by email.

Five steps to your first class

  1. Submit the Associate Information Form

    Tell us about your business, instructor qualifications and the courses you plan to teach. The form takes about three minutes.

  2. Sign the ALLCPR Partner Agreement

    We review your form and send the agreement and Fee Schedule for your review and signature.

  3. Complete the Red Cross agreement and account setup

    After signing with us, you complete the ALTP agreement directly with the Red Cross. This step usually takes one to two weeks, but the Red Cross decides whether and when to approve your account.

  4. Complete onboarding

    We arrange Partner Portal and class-registration access, provide instructions, and confirm roster submission, credit purchases, payment confirmation and support arrangements. We also review readiness for your first class.

  5. Buy credits and teach

    Begin only after your Red Cross ALTP agreement is effective, your account configuration is verified, onboarding is complete and first-class readiness arrangements are approved. Buy course-specific credits before teaching and allow time for payment confirmation. After class, submit your roster through the required systems.

Already a Red Cross LTP

With your permission, we ask the Red Cross how a switch would work for your account and provide a written plan. We do not request the change until you approve that plan in writing. A provider may hold only one Red Cross agreement type at a time; the Red Cross controls the transition, and we cannot guarantee there will be no gap in record entry.
