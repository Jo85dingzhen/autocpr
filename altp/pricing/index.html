Plan and setup fees

One-time setup fee: $199, waived for Founding Partners who sign the Partner Agreement on or before December 31, 2026.

Monthly Plan fee: currently estimated at $25; waived through December 31, 2026 for Founding Partners. Billing starts with the first full month after account activation, and no earlier than January 1, 2027 for Founding Partners. The fee applies even in months when you teach no classes.

No separate membership fee, fee per instructor, class minimum or termination fee. We give at least 30 days’ written notice before fee changes. Purchased credits are not repriced.

Optional services

Same-day record entry: $10 per class, only after we accept your request in writing. A complete, verified roster, applicable paid credits and the rush fee must be confirmed by noon Pacific on a staffed business day for an accepted 5 p.m. target. A request or payment alone does not secure acceptance. The rush fee is refunded within five business days if we do not accept the request or an accepted request misses its target.

Certificate corrections: no handling fee from ALLCPR. We submit complete correction requests to the Red Cross within two business days; the Red Cross makes the correction and controls its completion time. Any Red Cross charge caused by an error in your roster is passed on at cost; ALLCPR pays charges caused by its own error.

Your own website, training equipment and AEDs: quoted on request.

How prepaid credits work

Buy credits for a specific course in the Partner Portal before teaching, starting from one credit. Credits become usable only after payment is confirmed as received; a pending transfer or card authorization is not enough. There is no automatic replenishment.

Each attendee uses one credit, including unsuccessful or not-evaluated attendees; only successful students receive certificates. No-shows and cancelled classes use no credits. Credits pay for administrative processing and are not certificates or teaching authorization.

Credits are valid for the purchased course version until that version is retired, and for no more than five years after purchase. They do not transfer to another course or organization and do not convert to a new course version.

Credits are generally nonrefundable, including when you leave. Exceptions are duplicate or erroneous charges; ALLCPR ending the agreement without cause; the end of ALLCPR’s Primary agreement; the Red Cross ending your association for a reason not caused by you; or the Red Cross discontinuing a course without a replacement. The Partner Agreement sets the applicable refund terms.

Costs you cover separately

Student acquisition, Class Posting listings and other advertising, student-payment processing fees, liability insurance, equipment, venue, travel, supplies, applicable taxes, and your own teaching and administration time.

Leaving the program

You may give written notice at any time, with no termination fee or penalty. No monthly Plan fee is charged for a month that begins after your notice; a partly used month is not refunded. Ending takes effect at least 30 days after ALLCPR notifies the Red Cross, which we do within two business days of your notice.

Purchased credits may be used for classes taught before your end date and rosters submitted within 30 days after your notice. Credits still unused after those 30 days expire, subject to the agreement’s refund exceptions. Final statements, pending records and separation in the Red Cross system may take additional steps.
