What is AUTOCPR?

AUTOCPR is an AI-powered, low-staff community CPR training center developed by ALLCPR.

Each center combines an AI Agent operating platform, smart manikins and a standardized teaching process to deliver CPR, AED, First Aid and BLS training and certification. AUTOCPR is a training center, not a mechanical CPR device.

Who runs AUTOCPR

ALLCPR, a U.S. CPR training network founded in 2022 and based in San Jose, California. ALLCPR reports 160+ training centers in 35+ states, more than 100,000 people trained, and about 73,000 certifications in 2025.

ALLCPR delivers American Red Cross programs within the Red Cross PLTP partnership framework and AHA BLS Provider courses.

Where AUTOCPR is open

AUTOCPR smart manikin training runs in San Jose, Santa Clara and Newark, California. Santa Clara and Newark opened on June 1, 2026. San Francisco's first ARC AUTOCPR center is in development.
