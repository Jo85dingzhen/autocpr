How an AUTOCPR class works

You register and study online, then finish with a hands-on skills session on smart manikins.

The smart manikin system records your performance and gives feedback during practice. After you pass the skills evaluation, you receive certification under the rules of your course.

The four steps

  1. Register and pick a time

    Sign up and schedule your session on the digital platform.

  2. Complete the online part

    Do the course's online coursework before you arrive.

  3. Practice on smart manikins

    An intelligent training system guides the in-person skills session, records your compressions and gives personal feedback.

  4. Pass the skills check and get certified

    Certification follows the standards of the course. ALLCPR courses issue same-day or next-day eCards, depending on the course.
